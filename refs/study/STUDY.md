## Findings

Composition: the genre agrees on a dollhouse cutaway. The building is a stack of open rooms with the near wall and roof removed, so every room reads at once and the couch is always visible. Rooms are flat colour fields (one floor pattern, one wall colour) with a few big props; characters are fat, short-limbed and round-headed so they read at a distance. Wes Anderson framing adds symmetry: a centred, frontal, head-on view of the whole block, rooms as a grid of equal-sized boxes, a stairwell as a vertical spine. Camera: Moving Out uses a high, fixed-ish three-quarter top-down view that frames the whole play space, with a slow pan following the carriers; the delivery game uses a loose third-person chase view that swings and clips. For this game a side-on or very slight three-quarter orthographic-feeling cross-section that tracks up the floors suits both the cutaway and the print look. Lighting and colour: the references use soft, even, mostly unshadowed light with colour doing the work; the riso restyle should go further with no real lighting, just two or three flat ink tones per object (pink, teal, yellow, navy keyline) on off-white paper, shading by halftone dot size, shadows as a second ink at multiply, overlaps of pink and teal giving a dark purple-navy, yellow and pink giving orange. Production design: wallpapers, tiled bathrooms, wood floors, rugs and polka dots (all seen in the frames) become ink patterns; each floor gets a different pattern and one hero ink; the couch is the one object in a contrast ink (hot pink) so it stays findable; Mini Metro-style simplicity (circles, rounded rects, thin consistent line weight) keeps the shapes clean. Motion: carrying is loose and physical, with limbs and the load swinging, bumping walls and doorframes, and objects knocked about; the fun is two bodies steering one long rigid object round corners and stairs, with squash, wobble and recoil. In the print look, use stepped or stuttering animation lightly and let misregistration shift a few pixels on impact. Sound: from trailer and gameplay reading (no transcripts pulled), light upbeat jazzy or ukulele-style music, soft cartoon thumps and clunks, comedic grunts and yells, a satisfying chime for delivery; for the print look add paper rustle, stamp and press thunks, with a short music loop. Motifs: stairs, doorframes, the couch jammed at a landing, polka dots, checker tiles, floor numbers like signage, a timer pill and an objective line, a toppled lamp. Materials: nothing glossy; everything is matte paper, ink and cardboard. Halftone dots for tone, ink overprint by multiply blend, 1 to 3 px misregistration between ink layers, speckled grain and uneven ink coverage over a warm off-white paper, a navy keyline slightly offset from its fills.
