# Look: Couch Up The Stairs
## Palette
wall plaster #b99f7e, lower wall dirty green #5b6650, brick #b75129, door/rail wood #6b3f22, couch #c44d22 (hero, warmest object), bulb #fefddf, void #231c24, UI #fff4dc on #2a1a12   (refs/01.jpg style match, sampled)
## Lighting
Bare warm bulb per landing (point light, warm #ffd9a0), warm hemisphere fill, soft shadow key from camera side, dark void outside the shaft.   (refs/01.jpg mood match)
## Materials
Matte, flat/low-poly, canvas-painted grime and peeling plaster; brick patches; wood boards.   (refs/01.jpg style match)
## Shape language
Chunky chibi characters (big head, hood), rounded fat couch, thick banisters; exaggerated wobble.   (refs/01.jpg)
## Camera
Perspective fov 38, ~40° down from the open (+z) side, follows couch; near wall absent (cutaway), ceilings above never block.   (refs/01.jpg layout inspiration)
## UI and type
Chunky rounded cartoon font, top-left timer/floor, tilt meter top centre, warm cream on dark brown.   (builder's call)
## Sound mood
Loud synthesized cartoon thuds, grunts, oofs.   (builder's call)
## Assets
style: low-poly · all code-built, no files
## Avoid
Cold colours, dark murk over the couch, walls hiding the couch.
## Checks
1. Couch is the warmest, most saturated object in the opening look.
2. Players and couch visible and un-occluded at 1280x800.
3. HUD top-left and tilt meter readable.
